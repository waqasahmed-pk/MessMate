# Pastel Wallet 🌸

Pastel Wallet is a beautifully designed, high-end expense tracking application. Built with a stunning glassmorphism aesthetic and a pastel pink theme, it allows users to elegantly manage their monthly budget, track expenses across customizable categories, and visualize their spending.

## ✨ Features

- **No Installation Required:** The entire application (React, Tailwind styling, and icons) is packaged into a single, standalone `.html` file. Just double-click and use!
- **Glassmorphism UI:** Features premium frosted-glass effects, elegant sliders, and clean typography using the Inter font.
- **Local Storage Persistence:** All your salary data, categories, and logged expenses are safely saved to your browser's local storage. You won't lose data when you refresh!
- **Customizable Categories:** Add, edit, or delete budget categories on the fly. Each category is automatically assigned a vibrant pastel color.
- **Detailed Expense Logging:** Log individual expenses with specific dates, descriptions, and amounts.
- **Smart Budgeting:** Quickly adjust your monthly salary (e.g., +5k, -5k), view allocated vs. remaining funds, and track your total utilization percentage.
- **Visual Distribution Chart:** An interactive, animated SVG donut chart that visually breaks down your spending by category.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or save the `ExpenseTracker.html` file to your computer.
2. Double-click the file to open it in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox).
3. **Set your Salary:** Enter your monthly income at the top.
4. **Allocate Budgets:** Use the sliders or text inputs to allocate funds to different categories.
5. **Log Expenses:** Click the **"Details"** button on any category to open the breakdown window and log your daily expenses.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **HTML5 / CSS3:** For structure and custom slider styling.
- **React 18:** Handled via CDN for reactive UI components and state management.
- **Tailwind CSS:** Handled via CDN for rapid, responsive utility-class styling.
- **Babel (Standalone):** Compiles JSX directly in the browser.
- **Lucide Icons / Custom SVGs:** Lightweight and crisp vector icons.

## 📝 Notes

Because this application runs entirely in your browser using local storage, your data remains completely private to your device. Clearing your browser's cache/local storage will reset the app to its default state.
